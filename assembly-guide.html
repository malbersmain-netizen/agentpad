How it fits together

Read this before the step-by-step build. It answers "what am I actually making?"

The one-sentence version: there is a single perfboard; the LEDs and buttons solder to it and poke up through holes in the printed face plate; the ESP32 plugs into sockets on it; the LCD screws to the face plate and reaches the board with four wires; the face screws onto the printed box with the board sandwiched between.
