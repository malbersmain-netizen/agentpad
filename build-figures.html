Agent Pad — illustrated build

Figures for the case and every solder step. Drawn to scale where it matters.

Two changes before you print: open the middle band to ~30mm so the ESP32 can sit coplanar (stacked it needs 32.7mm and won't fit in 28mm), and cut the LCD window at 64.5 × 16mm — not the module's 80 × 36mm outline, or it drops straight through. Mount holes are M3 on ~75 × 31mm centres.
